About

I trained in forensic science at Lovely Professional University, where my coursework covered molecular biology, forensic serology, DNA profiling, crime scene investigation, forensic chemistry and toxicology, and medical law and ethics. My undergraduate research looked at forensic genetics and laboratory validation. In August 2026 I joined Truth Labs Forensic Services as a DNA Analyst. I care most about strict quality control, careful documentation, and results that are accurate and defensible.
